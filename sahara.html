The Sahara

Atlas passes, ancient kasbahs & golden dunes
Aug 27–29, 2026 · 3-day tour, 2 nights

The Great Road South

The classic 3-day overland route from Marrakech to Fes is one of Morocco's great road journeys. It climbs the High Atlas over the spectacular Tizi n'Tichka pass, drops to the fortified mud-brick kasbah of Aït Benhaddou and the film city of Ouarzazate, threads the dramatic Dades and Todra gorges, then reaches the towering Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga for a camel trek and a night under the stars. Day three crosses the Middle Atlas cedar forests to Fes.

This is the heart of our honeymoon. Book it as one private 3-day tour (covers Days 4–6 + both nights). Book the desert tour →

Key Stops Along the Way

◆ Tizi n'Tichka Pass

At ~2,260m, the highest major road pass in the High Atlas — switchbacks, Berber villages and sweeping views.

◆ Aït Benhaddou

A UNESCO World Heritage earthen ksar and the route's visual highlight — filmed in Gladiator, Game of Thrones and Lawrence of Arabia.

Must-see

◆ Ouarzazate

"Gateway to the desert" — home to Atlas Film Studios (one of the world's largest) and the restored Taourirt Kasbah.

◆ Dades Gorge

Rose-colored cliffs, the "Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs," and famous hairpin bends — our first overnight.

◆ Todra Gorge

A narrow canyon with sheer 300m walls and a river running through it; a lovely short walk.

◆ Erg Chebbi (Merzouga)

Morocco's iconic Sahara dunes, up to ~150m — sunset camel trek to a desert camp for tagine, drumming and stargazing.

Must-see

◆ Rissani & the Tafilalt

A bustling souk and palm oasis marking the historic edge of the trans-Saharan trade.

✡️ Jewish History of the Region

The desert's lost caravan communities

✡ Sijilmassa

Near present-day Rissani, this great walled oasis city (founded 757 CE) was the northern terminus of the western trans-Saharan caravan route — one of the medieval Maghreb's richest trade centers, with a significant Jewish merchant community before its decline.

✡ Tafilalt Ksar Communities

Jews settled across the Tafilalt and along the kasbah routes to take part in the caravan trade (gold, salt, dates), forming small communities throughout the valleys — historic now, the population long emigrated.

✡ Rissani Jewish Cemetery

A large burial ground outside Rissani's walls holds roughly 6,000 tombstones spanning about five centuries — a tangible reminder of the region's once-vibrant Jewish presence.

Note: These sites are largely historical and off the standard tour track — mention them to our driver if we'd like a brief stop near Rissani on the way to Merzouga.

Practical Tips

Long drive days: Marrakech→Dades and Dades→Merzouga are each several hours — break them up and don't underestimate the distances.

Late-August desert heat: travel early, do the camel trek at sunset, and confirm your desert camp tent has A/C (and a real bathroom) before booking.

Pack layers: desert days are scorching but nights can turn surprisingly cool, and the Atlas passes are chilly — bring a warm layer even in summer.
