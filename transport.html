The Big Picture

Our route is a clean one-way arc — no backtracking

Four travel legs connect our five stops. Two of them (the long ones through the desert) are wrapped into the 3-day Sahara tour with a private driver. For the rest, you can choose comfort (private car), value (train/bus), or speed (a short flight). Each leg below lists the real options with rough times and prices.

Honeymoon take: after a redeye and with luggage, private transfers are worth it for the door-to-door ease — but the Casablanca↔Marrakech train is genuinely pleasant and cheap if you'd rather save. Prices are approximate (≈10 MAD = $1); book trains/buses a few days ahead in peak season.
